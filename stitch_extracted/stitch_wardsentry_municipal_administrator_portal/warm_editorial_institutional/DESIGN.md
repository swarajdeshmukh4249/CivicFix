## Brand & Style

This design system blends institutional credibility with human warmth, tailored for global labor standards, humanitarian development, and social enterprise reporting. The aesthetic draws heavily from contemporary editorial design and civic modernism: purposeful, grounded, and structurally authoritative without feeling sterile or distant.

Visual themes center on solid earthy chromatic blocks, structured editorial compositions, crisp geometric containers, and sharp angled cuts that mimic architectural precision and editorial ribbons. The mood balances gravity and optimism—evoking trust, human dignity, cross-sector partnership, and tangible workplace progress.

## Colors

The chromatic structure establishes a grounded triadic balance between authoritative deep wine/plum, active terracotta/rust, and uplifting ochre/gold:

- **Primary (`#611E29` / deep wine):** Anchors major identity elements, navigation chrome, high-level headers, and key authoritative panels. Communicates institutional permanence and ethical gravity.
- **Secondary (`#EAAA0F` / warm gold/ochre):** Acts as the primary beacon for positive actions, energetic accents, metrics highlights, badge callouts, and key interaction cues.
- **Tertiary (`#C74724` / terracotta rust):** Used for urgent operational highlights, focus states, secondary tags, metric changes, and dynamic interactive elements.
- **Neutral & Surfaces (`#1B1B1B`, `#FAF8F5`, `#F5F1EB`, `#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast typography in espresso charcoal sits over warm cream and bone-white backgrounds, avoiding cold, clinical digital blues in favor of tactile editorial warmth.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes direct editorial communication and cross-cultural clarity:

- **Headlines (Chivo):** Crisp, geometric, and modern grotesque structure with confident punch. Used in heavy weights (`700`, `800`) to create stark editorial hierarchies and anchor high-level impact statements.
- **Body & Technical Copy (Noto Sans):** Selected for global multilingual legibility, neutral neutrality, and reading comfort across screens.
- **Labels & Overlines (Chivo / Noto Sans):** Rendered in uppercase with subtle tracking (+0.04em) on high-contrast badge containers, section kickers, and categorical metadata tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical 12-column responsive fluid grid that allows editorial blocks, image bleed panes, and high-impact metric containers to alternate cleanly down the page:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px outer margins. Content spreads often allocate 7 columns to content/narrative and 5 columns to data callouts, framed photo panels, or angled accent color blocks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px margins. Two-up module groupings collapse to stacked arrangements.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins. Angled accents and asymmetrical block offsets flatten into continuous vertical rhythm while preserving sharp contrast boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft drop shadows or artificial z-axis blurs. Depth is produced purely through **tonal layering and graphic stratification**:

- **Tonal Planes:** Surface depth relies on shifting between pure white (`#FFFFFF`), warm cream (`#FAF8F5`), and pale parchment (`#F5F1EB`), punctuated by full-bleed blocks of deep wine (`#611E29`) or vibrant ochre (`#EAAA0F`).
- **Low-Contrast Structural Borders:** Subtle dividing rules (1px solid `#E3DDD3`) organize dense tabular information without visual clutter.
- **Graphic Contrast Drops:** High-priority cards, floating status ribbons, and interactive banners use crisp, flat offset blocks or sharp borders rather than diffuse drop shadows.
- **Angled Geometry:** Accent ribbons, card header cuts, and feature banners use sharp chamfered or angled clip paths (e.g., `polygon(0 0, 100% 0, 100% calc(100% - 12px), calc(100% - 12px) 100%, 0 100%)`) to create tactile physical interest.

## Shapes

The design system employs an uncompromising **Sharp (`0`)** shape language:

- **Zero Corner Radius:** All buttons, cards, tags, banners, text fields, and structural modals feature strict `0px` border radii.
- **Angled Cuts:** Asymmetrical accent triangles and sliced polygon cuts (15° to 45° angles) serve as section transitions, ribbon tabs, and visual arrows pointing toward progress indicators and case studies.
- **Solid Geometric Dividers:** Heavy horizontal rules (2px–4px) in gold, terracotta, or wine establish unmistakable hierarchy across long-form reports.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** Strict 0px corners, solid Deep Wine (`#611E29`) background, crisp white Chivo uppercase text (`label-lg`), with a persistent 3px bottom accent rule or 45-degree corner notch. Hover state transitions background to Terracotta (`#C74724`).
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Solid Warm Ochre (`#EAAA0F`) background, charcoal text (`#1B1B1B`), shifting to deep gold (`#F2A900`) on hover with an active leftward inset shift.
- **Outline Button:** Transparent fill, 2px border in Deep Wine or Warm Ochre, high-contrast text, 0px radius.

### Chips & Status Badges
- High-contrast pill-free rectangular tags.
- Background in Ochre (`#EAAA0F`) with dark charcoal text for ongoing initiatives; Terracotta (`#C74724`) with white text for alerts/critical updates; Deep Wine (`#611E29`) for publication classification.
- Typography: Bold uppercase `label-sm` with tight horizontal padding (`0.5rem`) and zero rounding.

### Cards & Content Containers
- Zero radius, crisp 1px border (`#E3DDD3`) on warm cream background (`#FAF8F5`).
- Featured cards incorporate a top 4px solid accent bar in Ochre or Terracotta, or an angled corner ribbon badge spanning the upper right perimeter.
- Hover states employ an instant border change to `#611E29` and an interior background shift to pure `#FFFFFF`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Flat, rectangular fields with a light cream surface (`#F5F1EB`), 1.5px bottom border in `#333333` or 1px surrounding border.
- Focus state highlights with a 2px outline in Terracotta (`#C74724`) without glowing halos or soft shadow spreads.
- Inline error messages appear in Terracotta bold text with sharp warning indicator blocks.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square checkboxes (`0px` radius) with a 2px border in charcoal; checked state fills with Deep Wine and displays a crisp white checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature precise square-in-square nested targets or diamond indicators rather than soft circles, maintaining the structural geometric motif.

### Data Panels & Stat Callouts
- Large tabular modules displaying impact metrics featuring Chivo display sizes (`headline-xl`) paired with solid Ochre underline rules and small uppercase Noto Sans metadata tags below.
